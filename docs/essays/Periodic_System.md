# What Is New in the Periodic System?
### The Periodic Table as a Slice: A Hypothesis on the Multidimensional Architecture of Chemical Space
**Working Paper**

**Author:** Serge Magomet (Aimate)  
**Affiliation:** Independent Researcher, Ontology Lab  
**Year:** 2026  

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24582170.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24582170)

## Overview
This working paper distinguishes three levels often conflated in discussions of the Periodic System: established facts, an analytical thesis about the format of the table, and an empirical hypothesis about the structure of chemical space. It argues that the canonical two-dimensional table is a projection that omits the *conditions* (pressure, temperature, external fields) under which elemental properties are realized. By distinguishing between the configurational reading (an unconditional function of nuclear charge) and the behavioral reading (conditional on the medium) of an element's position, the paper proposes that chemical reality is better described as a multidimensional state space, of which the classical Periodic Table is merely a limiting, unmarked slice.

## Key Contributions
- **Analytical Thesis on Format:** Demonstrates that the canonical table's format inherently lacks the coordinate to express the *conditions* of manifestation, treating conditional behavioral properties as unconditional natural attributes.
- **Semantics of Position:** Clarifies the divergence between configurational and behavioral readings of an element's placement, showing how relativistic effects in superheavy elements expose this structural ambiguity.
- **Multidimensional State Space:** Proposes a formal framework for chemical reality with explicit coordinates: nuclear charge, composition, pressure, temperature, external fields, and time scale (kinetic coordinate).
- **Recontextualization of Anomalies:** Frames high-pressure phenomena (e.g., transparent insulating sodium, exotic stoichiometries, metallic hydrogen) not as exceptions to the Periodic Law, but as empirical pointers to the conditionality concealed by the 2D projection.
- **Computational Roadmap:** Outlines the methodological shift required to test the hypothesis of "periodicity along additional coordinates," advocating for integrating computational architectures trained on the state space rather than the classical table.
