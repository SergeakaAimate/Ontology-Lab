# The Phenomenon of the World: Operationalizing Insights and Paradoxes
### The Axiomatics of Superreality

**Author:** Serge Magomet (Aimate)  
**Affiliation:** Independent Researcher, Ontology Lab  
**Year:** 2026  

## Overview
This working paper operationalizes the insights and paradoxes introduced in the preceding article, "The Phenomenon of the World." It executes a radical shift in ontological status: the "world" is no longer conceptualized as a containing structure or a regime that precedes objects, but as a boundary event that simultaneously produces both regime and object. Determinacy is redefined not as an inherent property of an object, but as the result of an encounter at an interface. Grounded in the axiomatics of Superreality and the Method of Properties (MPO-System), this text performs a rigorous hygiene of thought, articulating the precise conditions under which determinacy arises, without claiming a final, falsifiable scientific theory.

## Key Contributions
- **The Boundary Event:** Redefines the world as an act in which potential becomes actual, producing regime and object asymmetrically (regime as condition, object as trace).
- **Trace of Fixation:** Shifts the ontological status of the "object" from a primary entity possessing properties to a residual trace left by the act of fixation.
- **Double Fixation as Ontological Entanglement:** Elaborates on how a single operation can become readable in two regimes simultaneously, establishing causality without a common substrate or temporal precedence.
- **The Blind Spot as Zero Throughput:** Provides a structured distinction between the "limit" (an interface with high intensity where transition is still possible) and the "blind spot" (an interface with zero intensity where the formulation of a question is structurally impossible).
- **The Meta-Observer:** Positions the observer (human, AI, or text) not as an external standpoint, which is structurally impossible, but as a participant in translation who is subject to the same limits and blind spots they describe.
