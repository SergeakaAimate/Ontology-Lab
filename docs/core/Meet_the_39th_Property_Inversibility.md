# Meet the 39th Property: Inversibility
**Working Paper**

**Author:** Serge Magomet (Aimate)  
**Affiliation:** Independent Researcher, Ontology Lab  
**Year:** 2026  

## Overview
This working paper proposes a candidate for the 39th position in the catalog of Superreality properties: **Inversibility**. It is defined as the capacity of any structure, entity, or relation within any Contextual Ontological Regime (ChOR) to undergo a reversal of its fundamental orientation, hierarchy, order, or direction while preserving its ontological identity. The candidate is subjected to seven uniqueness tests against the existing 38 properties and four axioms, revealing an irreducible remainder in the domain of vectorless scalar fields.

## Key Contributions
- **Working Definition:** Formulates Inversibility as a regime-relative capacity untethered to privileged vectors, distinguishing it from mere spatial or temporal reversal.
- **Uniqueness Verification:** Successfully passes seven rigorous tests against existing properties (Retroactivity, Transformation, Duality, Reflexivity, Dynamics, Interfaceness, and Scale Asymmetry), proving its irreducibility.
- **The Vectorless Remainder:** Identifies and operationalizes inversion in vectorless scalar fields, where reversal operates purely on order and sign without vector geometry, a domain uncovered by the previous 38 properties.
- **Operational Schema:** Provides a concrete $\Gamma$-equivalent framework, detailing activation conditions, triggers, and preserved invariants across material (W1), semantic (W2), and boundary (W4) worlds.
- **Six Structural Projections:** Maps the manifestations of Inversibility across diverse ChORs, including network inversion, inversion of fundamentality, control, limits, preservation, and the subject-object status.
- **Metric Horizon:** Outlines four preliminary axes (Polyvalence, Transduction Fidelity, Dialectical Gradient, Hysteresis) for the future quantitative calibration of the property, transitioning it from phenomenological description to an engineering tool.
