# The Phenomenon of the World: Matter and Thought
### A Working Paper on Distinction

**Author:** Serge Magomet (Aimate)  
**Affiliation:** Independent Researcher, Ontology Lab  
**Year:** 2026  

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23981923.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23981923)

## Overview
This working paper develops the implications of the ontology of fixation formulated in [The Phenomenon of the World](https://github.com/SergeakaAimate/Ontology-Lab/blob/main/docs/core/The_Phenomenon_of_the_World.pdf)
. It argues that the division of objects into "material" and "ideal" has no ontological basis: category membership is not a property of the object itself, but the result of an operation in which the object becomes determinate. Consequently, the ontological barrier between matter and thought cannot be fixed as a boundary. 

## Key Contributions
- **Suspension of Container Logic:** Demonstrates that the traditional mind–body problem presupposes a "container" logic that fails at both philosophical and empirical limits (e.g., quantum mechanics and cosmology).
- **Matter and Idea as Regimes:** Proposes that "matter" and "idea" do not denote two substances, but two distinct regimes of fixation, each with its own rules, medium, and boundaries.
- **Double Fixation:** Introduces a mechanism for inter-regime relations (e.g., qualia) not as a causal link between two substances, but as a mutual limitation of two fixations of a single operation.
- **Operational Boundaries:** Redefines the boundary between regimes not as an impenetrable wall, but as an operational condition that is established, crossed, and revised through specific acts of distinction.
